About

Nikhil Singhal designs, architects, and ships production AI systems for startups and enterprises. Agentic pipelines, retrieval and RAG, multi-agent orchestration, end-to-end AI product builds. The work spans active development, architecture advisory, and fractional CTO engagements.

Three decades of building came before the AI: enterprise scale at Microsoft, T-Mobile, AT&T, Expedia, and Hitachi Consulting. The last few years deep in AI products taught him one thing. The hardest problem is not what the model can produce. It is the gap between what the human means and what the machine does. He founded AI Trust Commons and authored the Human Intelligence Partnership Charter to work on that problem in the open.
